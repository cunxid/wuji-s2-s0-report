How to read a run. Every number below is read from the run's own folder: what the agent side recorded of each call (skill_calls/), the simulator's per-tick flight record (record.npz) and the host's verdict (evaluation/protocol.json). Each clip has three panels: (1) the depth image the skill was given, the robot's own pixels in blue, the planned palm path in green; (2) the plan: the robot alone, fingers as the plan commands them, moving through the boxes built from that depth; (3) the execution re-drawn from the recorded joints in the true scene, executed path in orange. Planned clearance is the smallest distance of the plan's rows to those boxes; when the planner stated only a lower bound (≥), the figure is this page's own replay of the saved rows. Executed clearance is the smallest signed distance in the true scene of the links that may never touch (arm, wrist flange, palm shell) and of the fingers to everything the call did not declare as a contact; a negative number is penetration. Contacts are the contact rows of the flight record during the call's ticks: NOT ALLOWED is a link that may never touch, NOT DECLARED is fingers on something the call named no contact with. A refused plan gives a still instead of a video.
