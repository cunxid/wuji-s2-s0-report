3 · Motion-planning gates around every skill call

A reference is only handed to S0 after passing, in order: (i) the yaw-sweep kinematic IK search (endpoint feasibility over candidate spawn yaws, warm-started damped-least-squares on a scratch model); (ii) the depth corridor check (swept path vs measured height map); (iii) the IK path gate: full-path 50 Hz IK with joint-velocity/acceleration bounds, manipulability (singularity proximity) and joint-limit margins — references that would demand accel spikes or pass near singular configs are rejected with a typed reason or returned with a minimal retime factor. A rejection is itself a boundary datum and is written to the atlas. Measured calibration (23 exact composed references on the shipped rig; thresholds = winner envelope +15%, no winner rejected by construction): the gate predicts 4 of 10 real failures before spending an episode — water-plant's 78° sustained orientation unreachability, pinch-tongs 428 mm out of workspace (57% solver-recovery dwell), the microwave press (375 mm/178°), and t09's overshooting aim whose wrist flange sweeps 73 mm into the table (vs 31 mm for the winning aim). 13/13 winners pass after the gate's one finding was FIXED at root cause: the press composer's frame anchor came from a sim-binding stub that free-fell through the floor during settle, so the authored march spanned metres of junk space (the episode survived only via the arrival-gated replan). The composer now detects the degenerate frame and re-anchors to the measured palm — the same episode passes better (15.4 mm vs 13.0) and its authored plan now passes the gate with 0 out-of-workspace frames; tracked arenas proven bit-identical to full float precision. A per-family view also separates the allen-key escape (21 mm/49° sustained hold error vs ≤0.1 mm for winning spins). Honestly reported blind spots: pure grasp-slips and semantic failures are kinematically clean — the gate cannot see them, the atlas can. Retime demo under a tight accel budget: t25 ×1.68, t09 ×1.07. The gate also works FORWARD as a planner: its swept-clearance table (4 candidate approaches, zero episodes) solved the t19 lying-cup approach graze — stock approach −12.9 mm penetration, gate-selected offset +29.6 mm clearance → the first 5/5 lifted acquires of that cup; and it measured that spawn-yaw CANNOT change the hand-vs-object approach (the clip anchors rigidly to the object) — the measured-offset CORRECT is the approach lever. API: ik_gate.check_reference(arena, poses_50hz, arm).

4 · The agent between skills — measured closed loop

After every verb segment the pipeline re-observes with the real perception stack (median 1.8 s per boundary), judges from perception only (ground truth recorded for honesty, never used to decide), and routes: continue · one measured-correction retry in the validated 12–80 mm band · typed abort.
