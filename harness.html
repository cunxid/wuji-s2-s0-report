3 · Motion-planning gates around every skill call

A reference is only handed to S0 after passing, in order: (i) the yaw-sweep kinematic IK search (endpoint feasibility over candidate spawn yaws, warm-started damped-least-squares on a scratch model); (ii) the depth corridor check (swept path vs measured height map); (iii) the IK path gate: full-path 50 Hz IK with joint-velocity/acceleration bounds, manipulability (singularity proximity) and joint-limit margins — references that would demand accel spikes or pass near singular configs are rejected with a typed reason or returned with a minimal retime factor. A rejection is itself a boundary datum and is written to the atlas. The gate's calibration study (thresholds fitted on winning vs failing references, with its pre-execution failure-prediction rate) publishes here when the run completes.
