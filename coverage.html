Environment fidelity audit

Per request, every result is labeled by how close its env is to the shipped benchmark arena. Exact (robot include swap only — the benchmark's own parameterization): t08 strike criteria (with the documented hammerS object-swap), scissors, glasses, tongs(spawn), ipad, microwave, mouse(base), hanoi, all press/actuate scenes. Inert tracked object added (cosim alignment requirement; parked far from the task): actuate/lpress scenes. Object-swap ports (V21 object injected because cross-object grasp transfer is the measured wall): t08 hammerS, bucket box60, handoff box60, analog rows. Scene-relocated (*_s2) — and WHY, measured: the original ball_to_bowl bowl sits at (0.14, 0.04) = 0.90 m from the shipped ROKAE base (reach ≈0.74 m) and 0.81 m from the Tianji shoulder; the original mouse at (0.6, 0.0) is 1.26 m out; the tongs settle on the floor at (0.49, 0.11, 0.02). These are DexJoCo legacy placements for +x-mounted Pandas — unreachable by construction for the shipped arms — so the s2 variants are the minimal relocation making those tasks physically posable, and results on them are labeled as such.
