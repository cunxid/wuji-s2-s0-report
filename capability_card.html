# Capability card: what the tracking policy can follow, and when each skill works

Read this before you plan. Assembled 2026-11-25 from probe data that already existed; nothing new was run for it.

Tags. `[measured n=..]`: this robot, these skills, the tracking policy ("ledger" = declared probe episodes judged from
the host's record; "live" = single sessions read afterwards; "rig double" = same arm and planner, no policy). `[prior:
training clips]`: inside what the policy saw in training (103 recorded picks); not "tracks". `[prior: old atlas,
unaudited]`: earlier sweeps with the same policy, graded by a height-only rule nobody has checked; a reason to try one
thing first, never a "can". `[untested]`: nothing on record. **Read every number as "n too small":** no cell has been
tried 8 times over 3 starts and 3 objects, and nearly everything AFTER the lift comes from one block.

## 0. Eight rules that would have prevented the known failures
1. **Grasp with a clip of the object's own shape, and check the thumb.** Call `propose_grasps` in a step of its own
   before `acquire`. Afterwards read `grip_opposed` and the fingertip forces. A grip without the thumb lost the object
   1.9 to 2.6 s after the lift in 6 of 6, whatever the hand was told `[measured n=6: 4 ledger, 2 live; one object]`;
   with its own clip the same object stayed 19 to 21 s `[measured n=3 live]`. "Own shape" is decided from the seen
   size, so a bar can get a cylinder's or a tool's clip: right after the lift 3.5 to 5 loaded fingertips went with an
   object that was kept, 1.1 to 3.3 with one that was lost `[measured n=12 ledger]`. Each offered grasp now says
   what kind of clip it is (`clip_source`, `trained`): clips the policy was trained on come first and kept their
   object 3 of 3, recorded but untrained clips 2 of 7 `[measured n=10 ledger]`; `acquire` warns when the bound clip
   is untrained. **Read `held` first:** `yes` = grip load AND seen in the hand; `no` = no grip load (an empty hand
   is no longer answered "unknown") or a loss (`dropped_during_...`); `unknown` = load but not seen: lift a little
   and look before you carry. A hammer handle was answered "held yes" in mid-fall once: when `grip_opposed` is
   False or the fingertips read about 1 N, wait 0.4 s and look again before the next skill `[measured n=1 live]`.
2. **One `firm` per grasp.** `acquire` ends with its own settle and `firm`; send no hand command afterwards. A second
   `firm` lost the object 11 s after the lift, frozen finger targets 14.6 s after it, and a harder squeeze pushed a
   thumbless grip out within 1 s `[measured n=1 each, live]`. `hold`, `transport`, `lift` send none.
3. **Set down before letting go.** Lower with `set_down(onto=...)` until the reply says `touch: felt`, then let go.
   Letting go in the air is a drop: from 2 cm up the object ended 30 to 32 mm away `[measured n=4 ledger; one
   object]`. A plain open, splay or cup threw it 7 to 21 cm `[measured n=3 live]`. **Even a set-down that says
   `felt` still lets go from 12 to 32 mm up** (the wrist flange stops the descent before the thing reaches its
   support) in 6 of 7, and where the thing ends grows with that height: a 60 mm cube moved 17 mm let go 12 mm up,
   35 mm from 34 mm, 60 mm from 59 mm; a block 3 to 6 cm; a 200 mm bar held standing fell over at every height
   `[measured n=15 ledger; four objects; thin]`. So today expect 2 to 6 cm of error where a thing is put, more for
   tall things: choose places with walls, a rim or room to spare, or finish with a fingertip push.
4. **Press shallow, then look.** Start at 3 mm, clear the view, look; go 5 mm deeper only if nothing changed. Presses
   of 3 to 10 mm threw a rocker switch in 5 sessions, 3 then 8 mm did not in 1, and 1 to 2 cm shoved its base aside in
   2 `[measured n=8 live; one kind of target]`. Never press again "to make sure".
5. **Only the tracking policy grasps (owner's rule, 2026-11-25).** The one way to take hold of a thing is `acquire`:
   the wrist goes down, then the thing and the wrist go up, and the policy moves the fingers. There is no scripted
   grasp and no finger closure of your own around a thing. The policy has almost no ball clips (9 of 3265) `[prior:
   training clips]`: push a ball, against something that stops it, or let it roll.
   **Dry runs are free:** `plan_motion` without `execute_plan` tells whether a pose or a whole multi-segment
   program of arm motion is accepted and how many rows each part takes; `solve_ik` and `propose_grasps` cost no
   ticks either. Measure and dry-run the whole plan before the first motion.
6. **Standing or lying decides the policy grasp.** A clip is used only if the object rests as it does in the clip
   (seen height within 12 mm or 15 %). Of 23 refusals of a rightly chosen object, 9 were `rest_pose_mismatch` and 11
   `no_feasible_candidate` `[measured n=23 ledger]`. Most objects have clips for ONE rest pose. Change the pose first
   `[untested]`, or change skill.
7. **Reach is the strip of table nearest the robot, about 0.30 m deep** (comfortable: 0.20 m deep, 0.45 m wide on the
   right arm's side); the far half is out of reach of both hands `[measured: rig geometry, computed]`. Check every
   goal with `solve_ik` first.
8. **Keep the palm 9 cm above any surface.** The wrist flange hangs 7 cm below a level palm and is the lowest part of
   the hand `[measured: rig geometry]`. Free moves to a palm-down pose under about 9 cm are refused; a carry asked to
   hover 6 cm over its goal was refused `[measured n=1 live]`. A thing whose middle is under 8 cm high is taken from
   above.

## 1. Trajectory envelope: what a skill's reference may ask of the policy
After the grasp the policy sees a frozen reference: only the palm's motion, the arm's error and physics reach it. Once
a clip is bound, a row with the fingers within 3 degrees of straight up or down is refused at plan time. Each line:
the skill's cap, the range in training clips, which of the two binds, what has been tried.
- **Palm speed, holding.** Cap 0.25 m/s (scripted grasp 0.12; empty hand 0.40); an argument can only slow it. The cap
  binds: clips reach 0.18 m/s (median clip), 0.37 (95th clip) `[prior: training clips]`. Level carries at 0.25 m/s
  kept the object 5 of 5 `[measured n=5 live; one object]`. On an arm, references of 0.42 to 0.50 m/s kept 11 to 12 of
  12 to 13 objects `[prior: old atlas, unaudited]`. First ladder on this rig (out-and-back 0.20 m at 0.12, 0.25,
  0.40, then about 0.48 m/s twice; rungs follow each other, so a late loss may be grip wear): a 60 mm cube kept
  with under 1 cm of shift at every rung; a five-sided block kept through the top rung in 2 of 4 ladders and was
  lost at 0.40 to 0.48 m/s in the 2 others; a 200 mm bar lying was never lost but walked 3 to 8 cm in the hand; a
  200 mm bar held standing was lost at 0.25 m/s `[measured n=7 ladders, ledger; thin]`. The arm itself draws at most
  about 0.5 m/s whatever is asked. The skills still cap at 0.25 m/s; only a program's own `MotionLimits` (all five
  values must be given) can ask for more. Compact = fast is fine; long = slow, and expect it to walk in the hand.
- **Palm acceleration, holding.** Cap 1.5 m/s^2 (scripted 0.5; empty 2.5). The cap binds: clips 1.0 (median clip), 1.8
  (95th) `[prior: training clips]`. Never varied `[untested]`.
- **Curvature and turn rate.** Cap 1.0 rad/s (scripted 0.5; empty 1.5). Nothing caps how tightly the palm's path bends
  (the other caps allow a 4 cm radius). Clips turn the wrist at 1.05 rad/s (median clip), 2.8 (95th) `[prior: training
  clips]`; recorded clips bend with a median path radius of about 11 mm and reverse 3.3 times a second. First ladder
  on this rig (policy grasps, own or own-class clips): arcs of 8 and 4 cm radius were carried with 2 to 18 mm of
  in-hand shift at 0.12 m/s and at the fastest the planner drew (0.17 m/s on an arc); arcs of 30 and 15 cm radius
  were refused by the planner as out of reach, not by the policy; three quick reversals: a block kept (16 mm), a
  cube kept (9 mm), a 200 mm bar held standing LOST `[measured n=8 ladders, ledger; thin]`. So a tight bend is not
  the risk; a long thing and a reversal are. On an arm, turns of 60 to 120 degrees at
  1.3 to 2.1 rad/s kept 10 to 11 of 12 to 13 objects; three quick 12 cm reversals kept 11 `[prior: old atlas,
  unaudited]`.
- **The FINGERS do not turn a held thing on command.** Asked 44 and 89 degrees about the vertical by turning the
  object's reference on a level, translating wrist (`transport(..., turn_by='fingers')`): the hold survived 6/6 and
  the wrist stayed level (<= 2.2 degrees), but the thing's heading at the opening was -1 to +32 degrees WHATEVER was
  asked (the zero-turn cells drifted as much as the turning ones); the rows the policy received were verified as
  written (bridge dump). What sets the heading is the in-grip roll at lift-off (37 to 50 degrees, 38 to 50 mm), not
  the reference `[measured 2026-11-25, n=1 per cell, one block, one grasp, exploratory]`. To turn a held thing, turn
  the WRIST with it: that delivered -73 of -90 once (17 off, same face) but may need another elbow posture and can
  roll the thing onto another face; the level wrist lands nearer (22 to 63 mm against 44 to 266) `[same study]`.
- **Tilt away from the grasp orientation.** Cap 120 degrees per call (scripted 30): defaults, not findings. Clips tilt
  81 degrees (median), 21 to 150 (most) `[prior: training clips]`. On this rig `[untested]`. Floating hand: about half
  kept at every angle from 15 to 150; pours of 105 to 135 degrees lost 4 of 4 `[prior: old atlas, unaudited]`. Any
  turn of a loaded hand is a risk: 30 degrees a step, and look.
- **Lift height.** Ask 0.05 to 0.10 m. Asked 0.10 m, the object rose 0.07 to 0.13 m `[measured n=12 ledger]`. Asked
  0.02 m, "held" could not be verified `[measured n=1 live]`. Clips lift 0.15 to 0.57 m: the skills lift less than
  training, not more `[prior: training clips]`. Above 0.10 m `[untested]`; 0.20 to 0.25 m kept 10 of 12 `[prior: old
  atlas, unaudited]`.
- **Carry distance.** 0.29 to 0.53 m kept 5 of 5 `[measured n=5 live; one object]`. Clips carry 0.18 m (median), 0.29
  (95th) `[prior: training clips]`. Reach binds first. Floating hand: 0.1 to 0.2 m kept four in five, 0.3 to 0.4 m
  half `[prior: old atlas, unaudited]`.
- **Going DOWN with a held thing.** A policy grasp that survived the lift and a level carry lost the block during a
  lowering of about 10 cm in under a second (12 cm in all, wrist down at up to 0.25 m/s): it crept 2 cm in the hand
  and left it 13 cm above the table,
  before any command to open `[measured n=1 live, one-shot run]`; the same loss was seen in three other set-downs
  today `[measured n=3 live; one object]`. A 2.5 cm lowering at 0.20 m/s kept it 4 of 4 `[measured n=4 live; one
  start, not blind]`; a 3.4 cm lowering at 0.05 m/s after a carry at lift height kept it and the place held `[measured
  n=1 live, one-shot run]`. Keep the lowering short: set the lift by the height of the place, carry level at that
  height (hover close above the place), then lower a few centimetres.
- **Hold time, palm still.** Outside training: no clip stands still for more than about a second `[prior: training
  clips]`. Six seconds after a 0.10 m lift: kept 7 of 13 (which: see Objects); every loss came within 2 s of the lift
  `[measured n=13 ledger; 9 objects, one start each]`. 19 to 21 s: kept 3 of 3 `[measured n=3 live; one object]`. The
  grip creeps 0.5 mm/s. Longer than 21 s `[untested]`. Your own thinking is a hold: up to 3 s per call.
- **Release mode.** Set down until felt, then open: the object moved 22 to 28 mm (3 times) or 50 to 66 mm and tipped
  (2 times) `[measured n=5 live; one object, held tilted]`; clips let go on the support and the object moves 3 mm
  `[prior: training clips]`. In place, in the air: a drop, 9 to 27 mm sideways plus the fall `[measured n=18: 4
  ledger, 14 live; one object]`. Opening time made no difference `[measured n=4 ledger]`.
- **Contact motions.** `stroke`, `slide`, `push` move at 5 cm/s. `strike` arrives at 0.25 m/s, at most 0.8 (scripted
  0.4) `[untested]`. **Empty hand:** 0.40 m/s, 2.5 m/s^2, palm error at most 4.4 mm `[measured n=12 live]`.
- **Objects.** Training: shortest side 12 to 60 mm, longest 42 to 200 mm, 25 to 160 g; prisms, cylinders, cubes, bars;
  no hammer, screwdriver, bulb or cup `[prior: training clips]`. Alone on an empty table, still held after 6 s: a
  five-sided block 54 x 57 x 70 mm on a side face, a cube 42 mm, a square bar 35 x 35 x 200 mm lying (twice), a cube
  60 mm (twice), a square bar 35 x 35 x 200 mm standing. Lost within 2 s of the lift: a three-sided bar 37 x 43 x 200
  mm lying (twice), a five-sided block 54 x 57 x 70 mm on a side face with another shape's default clip, an oval bar
  30 x 50 x 200 mm lying, a small hammer 23 x 117 x 205 mm, a light bulb 48 x 138 mm. Refused: a cylinder 50 x 70 mm
  upright, a cylinder 60 x 70 mm upright, a five-sided block 54 x 57 x 70 mm on its tall face (no_feasible_candidate);
  a short cylinder 60 x 42 mm flat face down (invalid_request); a three-sided prism 45 x 52 x 42 mm
  (own_shape_has_no_grasp) `[measured n=19 ledger; one start each]`. Earlier arm runs never took a 25 or 40 mm ball, a
  thin cylinder, a cone, a bulb, a disc, a mug, a pen or a plate `[prior: old atlas, unaudited]`.

## 2. Skill conditions
**Every skill** refuses an observation older than 150 ticks or taken before the arm moved. The head camera does not
see under the hand: `clear_view`, then look. Depth joins things that touch and misses painted marks (`point_at`).
- **`acquire`** (policy grasp, then lift). Works when one object stands free, its seen size matches a clip, it rests
  as the clip has it, and it is in reach. Of 52 asks on the right object 29 executed, and 19 of those were carried
  clear for a second `[measured n=52 ledger]`. Alone, with nothing else on the table: 13 of 19 executed, 7 still held
  after 6 s `[measured n=19 ledger]`. With default clips only, things 180 to 320 mm long: carried 0 of 9 (knocked or
  tilted) `[measured n=9 ledger]`. One clip per session: a second object cannot be grasped by the policy. Refusals:
  `rest_pose_mismatch` -> change its pose; `no_feasible_candidate` -> out of reach or crowded: move it nearer, or take
  another hand turn from `propose_grasps`; `own_shape_has_no_grasp` -> change its pose, push it, or do without holding it (there is no scripted grasp). Reports `held`,
  `grip_opposed`, fingertip forces; an empty hand is answered `held: no`; after a failed grasp or a loss `hold`, `lift`, `transport` and `set_down`
  are refused with the reason (raise an empty hand with `retreat`), and the skills that need an empty hand clear a
  stale belief themselves: confirm an `unknown` from a clean view before carrying
  `[measured n=1 live]`. The object can sit up to 9 cm to the side of the palm: expect centimetres of offset where it
  is put down `[measured n=1 live]`. A 40 x 200 mm cylinder with its own clip was grasped, carried and set down
  `[measured n=1 live]`.
- **`grasp_scripted`**: NOT for S2 any more (owner's rule: grasping is the policy's). Use `acquire`.
- **`hold`**. After an own-clip grasp, to about 20 s (see hold time). Sends no hand command and does not rescue a grip
  that slides. Reports `held`; `grasp_slip` when the hand is seen empty. **`lift`**: straight up with what is held;
  refused where a joint stands on its limit (ask for less); above 0.10 m with a load `[untested]`.
- **`transport`**. Carries to above a goal at 0.25 m/s; default hover 0.17 m (a low hover is refused, rule 8). Kept a
  block over 0.29 to 0.53 m `[measured n=5 live]`; a scripted grasp lost a ball in 3 of 3 `[measured n=3 live]`.
- **`set_down`**. `onto=` a spot on the table: the arm felt the table 4 of 4 `[measured n=4 live]`. `onto=` a named
  thing: ended 2.6 to 3.2 cm above it 4 of 4 before a fix; after it, felt a book once and pressed a paper curl twice
  without feeling it `[measured n=7 live]`. Without `onto` the object still hangs 2 to 4 cm up `[measured n=4 live]`.
  `touch: not_felt` means it did not arrive, or the thing is soft. It lost the object mid-descent once and said only
  `held: unknown`: look from a clean view after every `set_down`. The flange side of the hand must end over free space
  at the place, so choose the hand turn of the grasp for the place. Reach the hover with `transport` first: the
  straight approach grazed a neighbour `[measured n=1 live each]`.
- **`release_in_place`**. Opens where the hand is; moves the object 2 to 3 cm and lets a tilted one fall flat (see
  release mode). Reports `landed`. **`place_release`**: drops from 3 cm and raises the drop pose when blocked; its
  `open` and `splay` threw a light block 8 to 21 cm `[measured n=2 live]`. Prefer `set_down`.
- **`slide`** (fist, one straight stroke). Shut a drawer 0.135 m in one call `[measured n=1 live]`. A small free block
  was refused in both forms tried `[measured n=2 live]`; away from the robot is often out of reach.
- **`push`** (fist toward a point). A 60 mm block moved 27 mm for 60 asked `[measured n=1 live]`; a ball 79 to 89 mm
  for 80 asked `[measured n=3, no policy]`. Earlier arm runs: 31 of 77 ended on target, none with a sphere, cone,
  bulb, disc or cup `[prior: old atlas, unaudited]`. One stroke, no steering: expect to push twice.
- **`guarded_press`**. For something fixed that pushes back; depths in rule 4. A light or free thing is not felt.
- **`stroke`**. A 40 mm fingertip stroke ran and dragged a loose 80 g slab 10 mm `[measured n=1 rig double]`; with a
  held tool `[untested]`.
- **`strike`**, **`pour`**, **`wrist_reorient`** with a load, **`spin_in_hand`**: never run with the policy on this
  rig `[untested]`. Earlier arm runs of spins and pours mostly lost the grip `[prior: old atlas, unaudited]`. The
  nearest measured thing: a spin reference about the VERTICAL on a translating wrist did not turn the block (see the
  fingers bullet under trajectory parameters) — expect `spin_in_hand`'s turn not to be delivered either until measured.
- **`orbit`**. Turned a gripped hinged lid 66 degrees in 7.9 s `[measured n=1 rig double]`; refused whole if any part
  of the arc is out of reach. With a policy grasp `[untested]`.
- **`move_to_pose`**, **`retreat`**, **`go_home`**, **`clear_view`**. Empty-hand moves, reliable inside reach
  `[measured n=12 live]`. `retreat` and `go_home` are refused while closed fingers press something against the flange:
  open first. `clear_view` cost 116 to 257 ticks a look `[measured n=11 calls]`.

## 3. How to plan with this card
1. Write the whole plan first: per step the skill, the section 2 condition that holds, the parameter and its tag.
2. Choose skills whose conditions hold: object free, in the reach strip, resting as its clips have it.
3. Pick parameters inside measured lines; where only a prior exists take the cautious end. A step that rests on
   `[untested]` is a risk: keep it small, put it late, and write down what you do if it fails.
4. A grasp refused for its rest pose means: change the object's pose first, then ask again.
5. After two refusals for the same reason change the skill, not its arguments: an argument change went through 5 times
   in 51, a change of skill 7 times in 13 `[measured n=70 refusals]`.
6. Count ticks first: about 200 a call, 300 to 500 a grasp, 300 for the ending `[measured n=18 episodes]`.
7. With an object in the hand you have about 20 s (the longest hold on record; a call takes about 4 s plus up to 3 s
   of thinking): at most three calls between grasp and release; leave 3 cm of room at every release.
