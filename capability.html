Correction, 2026-11-23 — a success mark on this page means a score crossed its bar, and the audit found several of these scores unreliable. This page used to list these cells under “what works”, as the measured capability boundary. Every result the project had reported as a pass was audited on 2026-11-23. None of the 50 evaluation tasks was completed, and none of the 10 in-hand tasks was completed as described (audit of reported passes; record s2/s2loop/audit_2026-11-23.json). The audit also found that the scores behind those results did not measure what they claimed. The success and failure flags on this page were set by graders; for the verbs in this list they were set by those same scores, so their “scored as success” column is unverified:
  • place and the legacy place_* labels — “in bowl” is a single-point test on the object's tracked origin at the last tick. In an audited run it passed a hammer lying across the bowl rim with nothing in the bowl.
  • spin, spinplace and the inhand_rot_* labels — the rotation score is the unsigned angle between two palm-relative snapshots. It ignores which axis and which direction the object turned, so tumbles and uncommanded rolls count. “Held” is a height test, not a contact test.
  • pour — the tilt score is the absolute angle of the object's axis to vertical, not the change produced by the tilt.
  • strike — nail depth is the single lowest reading over the whole episode, with no check that the nail stays driven, when the reading happened, or that the hammer touched the nail. No “official” t08 label is supported.
  • press — one bar of 4 units of peak joint travel, written for the mouse button in millimetres. In the audited evaluation rows it was applied to hinge angles in milliradians and to loose bodies. On the mouse button itself the audit judged it a fair measure of “the button went down”. The press cells on this page are: mouse_button [tianji].
Not audited: the probe and probe_task cells, which record how closely the palm followed its reference (tracking error) and whether the object stayed lifted during the motion (grip retention, measured by object height). The hold, push and handoff cells were not audited either. The cells are shown as recorded; no atlas data was changed.

Cells that are the recorded run of an audited row show the audited verdict in place of a success mark (7 on this page: 2 DEMO, 5 FAIL). FAIL means the result came from a motion the plan did not command, or the score did not measure what the row claimed. DEMO means the commanded motion did happen, but with a substitute object, a marker instead of a real target, or a generic scene — a skill demonstration, not a completed benchmark task. 3 more success cells returned exactly the same scores as one of those runs; they are marked as the same episode, not as a separate result.

Criterion text marked “as registered” is copied from the verb registry (s2/dexharness/registry.py), which the audit did not change. Where the audit does not support that text, the note under the verb says so.
