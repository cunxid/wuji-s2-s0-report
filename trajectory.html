The generated trajectory in 3D: what transport hands to S0

What this is. One transport call generates one reference: a pose of the THING and a pose of the WRIST for every row (one row = 20 ms), with a phase name. Left: that generated reference alone. Right: what the arm and the tracking policy S0 actually did on the same rows, with the generated tracks faint behind. Both views share one camera and one slider. In "compare" mode both sides are generated references: linear against spline through the same keypoints, or the wrist turning with the thing against the wrist only translating (same keypoints; the thing's reference turns and the fingers are asked to do the turn).

What is exact and what is not

  • Tracks and poses are exact. The thing track and the wrist track are the rows the skill stored (diagnostics.reference: ref_thing, ref_wrist); nothing is reconstructed. The stored wrist rows were checked against the commanded palm in each record.
  • The thing is the scene's own visual mesh of the block (16 triangles in the first call listed), not simplified.
  • The hand is the robot model's own hand, simplified. each link's visual mesh replaced by the convex hull of its points snapped to a 2.5 mm grid: 1210 triangles in all, from 113886 in the model. Hulls fill in hollows, so the palm looks fuller than it is.
  • The thing's frame. The stored thing pose is the pose of the thing's own frame: its centre, with the rotation counted from how it lay at the start. The scene's body frame of the block is a fixed step away from it (about 14 mm and a quarter turn). That step is taken at the first row, where the block lies still, and used to place the mesh and to turn the recorded block poses into the same frame. So "commanded against actual" is 0 at the first row by construction.
  • The reference has no hand in it. It holds two tracks: the pose of the thing and the pose of the wrist. On the left the dark body is the palm drawn at the commanded wrist pose, so its orientation can be read; no fingers are drawn, because the reference holds none: the tracking policy moves the fingers from the thing's track.
  • On the right the fingers move with the recorded joint angles (forward kinematics of the 20 joints; against the recorded link positions it is within 0.01 mm).
  • Keypoints are the call's own when it stored them (the ablation episodes), each marked at the nearest row of the track; for the two earlier runs, which stored none, the start, the highest row and the last row of the move are marked instead.
  • n = 1 per call. Development runs.
