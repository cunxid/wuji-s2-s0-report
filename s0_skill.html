The one skill

whowhat
The agent givesthe thing, and 5 or 6 sparse keypoints: where the thing's middle should be along the way, from where it stands to where it should rest. Nothing else: no grasp, no hold, no speed, no segments.
The skill doeschooses the grasp the policy makes, for the whole way (a hand that can follow every keypoint and leaves no finger under the thing where it lands); chooses the hold from what was measured; knows where the thing sits in the hand; turns the keypoints into ONE curve with a time law inside what S0 can track (speed, turning, no stop with a loaded hand); checks reach and collisions against the depth image; lands under a guard; opens; leaves.
S0 getsone continuous reference: the thing's track and the wrist's track, from one curve. The grasp itself is S0's own.
The agent gets backwhat the senses show: was the thing held to the release, where it landed, what was changed in its keypoints and why, or why nothing moved.
