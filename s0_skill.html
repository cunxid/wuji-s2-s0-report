How the capability atlas is used. It answers one query, query(segment, object, hold): can (with numbers), cannot (measured), mixed, or untested. It moves nothing and is never copied into a skill or a prompt. Three consumers:

  1. The card in S2's briefing: generated from the admitted rows, short enough to sit whole in a one-shot briefing, so that the plan S2 writes already respects what was measured.
  2. The query that shapes proposed keypoints: s0_skill.propose turns the rows' numbers into the keypoints' shape (where the thing is released, a spacing that implies a trackable speed, no reversal with a loaded hand, where the thing sits in the hand).
  3. The gate between plan and execute: the commit (and check, identically) queries every stretch of the one reference. Three levels: report (untested: say so and run), limit (a measured bound: slow the time law to it and say so), refuse (nothing moves; the answer is the nearest feasible plan). It limits or refuses only on a measured cannot, never on untested.

From project_description.md, sections 1 to 3. S0 never sees the task, the keypoints or the scene: only the reference. Only s0_skill(plan) moves the robot.

The one skill

whowhat
The agent givesthe thing, and 5 or 6 sparse keypoints: where the thing's middle should be along the way, from where it stands to where it should rest. Nothing else: no grasp, no hold, no speed, no segments.
The skill doeschooses the grasp the policy makes, for the whole way (a hand that can follow every keypoint and leaves no finger under the thing where it lands); chooses the hold from what was measured; knows where the thing sits in the hand; turns the keypoints into ONE curve with a time law inside what S0 can track (speed, turning, no stop with a loaded hand); checks reach and collisions against the depth image; lands under a guard; opens; leaves.
S0 getsone continuous reference: the thing's track and the wrist's track, from one curve. The grasp itself is S0's own.
The agent gets backwhat the senses show: was the thing held to the release, where it landed, what was changed in its keypoints and why, or why nothing moved.
