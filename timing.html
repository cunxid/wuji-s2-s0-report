Honest architecture note. In this architecture the LLM (S2) composes plans and routes retries between episodes; its runtime decision policy during an episode is the typed actor (_route, sub-second), while open-ended LLM reasoning happens at plan-authoring time. So time-to-success — which includes every failed attempt, summed from the recorded attempt histories (episode walls estimated from run-dir timestamps, hence "est") — is the honest "agent in the loop" cost, and the single-episode staged end-to-end is the deploy-time cost of a calibrated task. No LLM latencies are fabricated or included. Further method notes: alias bench rows (hammer_nail = t08, click_mouse = t14, pick_bucket = t26) carry the same builder, scene and winning spec in eval_results_v2 and share one measurement (their search histories are their own); execute includes cosim process start + model load exactly as the table below does; arena load + 250-step settle (sim bring-up, ~0.2–0.4s) is reported in the JSON but not counted in perceive; scene files written by the re-run plan stage were snapshotted and restored so execute replays the bit-identical recorded scene; the production loop also re-observes after each episode to self-judge — budget one extra perceive pass per attempt; in-hand capture-basin qualification episodes (shared per object across tasks) are not counted in per-task time-to-success. Full per-call breakdown: s2/s2loop/pipeline_timing.json.
