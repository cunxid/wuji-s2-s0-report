What these numbers are. In this architecture the LLM (S2) composes plans and routes retries between episodes; its runtime decision policy during an episode is the typed actor (_route, sub-second), while open-ended LLM reasoning happens at plan-authoring time. Attempts counts every attempt up to and including the run that was reported as the pass, and time spent sums their wall-clock from the recorded attempt histories (episode walls estimated from run-dir timestamps, hence "est"). This page used to head those columns "attempts→success" and "time-to-success", and to call the staged end-to-end figure the deploy-time cost of a calibrated task. Those descriptions are withdrawn: the audit found that no run completed its task, so the figures are the cost of the attempts and of one staged run. No LLM latencies are fabricated or included. Further method notes: alias bench rows (hammer_nail = t08, click_mouse = t14, pick_bucket = t26) carry the same builder, scene and recorded spec in eval_results_v2 and share one measurement (their attempt histories are their own); execute includes cosim process start + model load exactly as the table below does; arena load + 250-step settle (sim bring-up, ~0.2–0.4s) is reported in the JSON but not counted in perceive; scene files written by the re-run plan stage were snapshotted and restored so execute replays the bit-identical recorded scene; the production loop also re-observes after each episode to self-judge — budget one extra perceive pass per attempt; in-hand capture-basin qualification episodes (shared per object across tasks) are not counted in per-task time spent. Full per-call breakdown: s2/s2loop/pipeline_timing.json.

The baseline rows were failures already and were not re-examined in the audit.
