FAIL means the result came from a motion the plan did not command, or the score did not measure what the row claimed. What was wrong with the scoring

  • official_check (s2/s2loop/eval48.py) fills predicate terms it cannot measure with fixed passing values; no official label is supported
  • press score: a 4-unit bar written for a mouse button in millimetres was applied to hinge angles in milliradians and to loose bodies
  • in-hand rotation score: unsigned angle between two palm-relative snapshots, blind to axis and sign, so tumbles and uncommanded rolls count
  • nail depth: single lowest sample over the whole episode, with no persistence, phase or contact check
  • in-bowl: single-point test on the tracked body origin at the last tick
  • pour tilt: absolute angle of the object axis to world up, not the change produced by the tilt
  • several rows were scored by builder-local checks with literal values rather than by a repo grader
