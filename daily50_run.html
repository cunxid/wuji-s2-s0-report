Every episode is listed, whatever its verdict; FAIL, FLAGGED and NOT_EVALUABLE test episodes are in the denominator. A test episode is left out of the rate, and named above with the reason, when its files do not hold together: the card's verdict is not the one the scoring command wrote, the rule that judged it or the scene the simulator recorded is not the task's, or the task's scene, rule or description changed after the episode.
