## Supported

### S1. Measure, then dry-run the whole program
- WHEN: before the first motion, and before any program of your own (grasp, carry, press, stroke): poses, hand turn and heights are still guesses.
- DO: spend zero-tick steps first: `point_at` grids, `propose_grasps` in a step of its own, `solve_ik` sweeps over hand turn and height (a refusal names the nearest link and its millimetres: a free ruler), then `plan_motion(view, segs)` WITHOUT `execute_plan`, in a step of its own; execute the same segments in the next step, before the hand has moved.
```python
p = plan_motion(view, segs)                       # dry run: nothing moves, 0 ticks
result = {"ok": p.ok, "rows": p.diagnostics.get("rows"), "why": None if p.ok else p.error.message[:300]}
```
- CHECK: `p.ok` and rows that fit the budget; after `execute_plan(p)`: `ok`, and `steps_executed` equal to the rows.
- EVIDENCE: worked in 8 of 8 trials on 7 tasks: a program accepted in a dry run ran to its end (t49/40, t50/40, t40/40, t40/41, t02/40, t03/40, t34/40, t48/40); both ball carries that passed ran at the first try after zero-tick steps only. Dry runs threw out a fist tap (t03/40), a side approach (t42/41) and an uphill push (t48/40) for 0 ticks. Traps: dry runs in the SAME step as an execute cost the 150-tick latency cap; a dry run made before the hand moved over the target did not carry over (both t34/40). **STATUS: supported**
### S2. Choose the grasp by the place
- WHEN: the thing must end on, in or over something else, and that place has walls, neighbours or a raised edge, or lies far from the robot.
- DO: before grasping ask `solve_ik` for the hover and the touch height at the PLACE with the palm orientation each feasible grasp ends in (position and quaternion in `grasp.diagnostics['palm_at_f_cut']`); keep a grasp whose wrist-flange side ends over free space there; if none reaches, change skill (scripted grasp, pinch, push) before a tick is spent. `ok = solve_ik(view, Pose(place_xyz, quat_of_grasp, 'world'), hand='close').ok`
- CHECK: the carry reaches the hover it was planned for; the set-down reports no `line_capped_m` of centimetres.
- EVIDENCE: worked 6 of 6 on 6 tasks: the place was reached as the zero-tick check said (t18/40, t19/40, t49/40, t50/40, t42/40, t34/40). Skipped and paid for in 3 trials (t27/0: flange over the target, descent capped 5.8 cm; t21/0: set-down refused over an occupied holder; t28/0: a 426-tick push before any check). In t32/0 the check showed the destination out of reach of the only usable hand: nothing to carry there. **STATUS: supported**
### S3. Grasp the part, not the cluster
- WHEN: the thing to take touches or stands on another thing (a cloth, a plate, a board, other tools): the marked picture gives one big box, words return the whole cluster, `propose_grasps` answers `no_grasp` or offers a clip for another rest pose.
- DO: take the thing's own mask part from `numbered` (its label contains 'mask part'; choose by place and size) and ask for grasps on that part in a step of its own; the part is also the target for `grasp_scripted`. `parts = [(n, o.label) for n, o in numbered.items() if o.label and 'mask part' in o.label]; g = propose_grasps(view, numbered[PART])`
- CHECK: `g.grasps` is not empty; each grasp's `diagnostics['shape_match']` and `['trained']`; the part has the thing's size, not the cluster's.
- EVIDENCE: worked 2 of 2 on 2 tasks: asked on the part, feasible grasps came back (t19/40, where the grasp then held, PASS; t49/40); a scripted grasp also took a tool by its handle part (t02/40). Asked on the cluster or by words: `no_grasp` or a wrong clip in 5 trials on 4 tasks (t19/0, t40/40, t40/41, t03/40, t27/40). **STATUS: supported**
### S4. Policy grasp: ask first, read the grip, carry level at once
- WHEN: one free thing with a feasible clip as it rests (its own shape, or another shape the provider offers) must be lifted and carried.
- DO: `propose_grasps` in a step of its own; prefer a trained clip whose `palm_at_f_cut` lies over the thing's middle (a long thing gripped at one end pivots out) and whose `raise_m` is 0; ask listed size alternatives by name (`object_class=`) BEFORE binding: one clip per session. `acquire` in the next step with `lift_m` 0.06 to 0.10; then ONE level `transport` in the grasp's own orientation: no hand command, no turn, no waiting. `r = acquire(view, obj, grasp=g.grasps[0], lift_m=0.08); f = r.execution.final_observation.robot_state.fingertip_force_n['right']`
- CHECK: `held: yes` (place seen empty, thing seen in the hand) with `grip_opposed` True. With `grip_opposed` False go on only if at least three fingertips read 2 N or more, and at once; fingertips near 1 N: `hold(view, duration_s=0.4)` and look again. `capture_missed` with loaded fingers: one end still stands on the support; do not lift it yourself.
- EVIDENCE: worked 11 of 16 on 8 of 12 tasks: lifted and still in the hand at the next skill (t27/0, t27/3, t27/20, t27/40, t35/0, t33/0, t34/40, t42/40, t21/0, t32/0, t19/40; not t19/0, t38/0, t02/40, t48/40, t01/40). `grip_opposed` True: 9 of 9, two of them other-shape clips (t21/0, t32/0). False: kept 2 of 4 (t19/40, t42/40: fingertips of 2 to 7 N; t19/0 empty; t02/40 falling at about 1 N). `transport` right after the grasp kept the thing in 7 of 9 calls on 4 tasks; lost: a 3 cm rod (t33/0), a block after a quarter turn (t27/40). **STATUS: supported**
### S5. Believe the fingertips and the picture, not the held word

- EVIDENCE: worked 3 of 4 on 4 tasks: read at once, the forces gave the true state (t27/40: empty after a carry; t34/40: empty after a lift; t18/40: still held although the skill said slip). ### S6. One-plan pinch for a ball
- WHEN: a ball or round fruit of 6 to 7 cm on a low support (a stand, a plate); `propose_grasps` gives `no_grasp` or only an untrained clip; the scripted grasp does not hold balls.
- DO: ONE plan, dry-run first (S1): open hand straight down, palm origin 7.5 cm behind the ball's centre along the fingers and 2.5 cm above its top; a dwell that closes and keeps `'close'` (no `firm`); lift; a level linear carry without a pause; `'open'` over the goal; rise; a free move back to the start joints. Aim the let-go with the ball's centre 5 cm ahead of the palm origin and 2 to 3 cm to the thumb side of the goal, or with a rim on the little-finger side.
```python
c = numbered[N].point_cloud; q0 = view.robot_state.arm_joint_positions['right']      # N: the object that holds the ball
segs = [MotionSegment('free', goal=palm_down_pose(pre, yaw), hand=HandCommand('open', ramp_ticks=30)),
        MotionSegment('linear', goal=palm_down_pose(grasp, yaw), exclude=c, contact_links=('fingers',)),
        MotionSegment('dwell', duration_ticks=65, hand=HandCommand('close', ramp_ticks=40), exclude=c, contact_links=('fingers',)),
        MotionSegment('linear', goal=palm_down_pose(up, yaw), exclude=c, contact_links=('fingers',)),
        MotionSegment('linear', goal=palm_down_pose(over_goal, yaw)),
        MotionSegment('dwell', duration_ticks=30, hand=HandCommand('open', ramp_ticks=5)),
        MotionSegment('linear', goal=palm_down_pose(above, yaw)), MotionSegment('free', goal=q0)]
p = plan_motion(view, segs); e = execute_plan(p) if p.ok else None
```
- CHECK: the step's own picture (arm parked): the support is empty, the ball lies at the goal; the same measurement after a dwell of 2 to 3 s says it is at rest.
- EVIDENCE: worked 2 of 2 on 2 tasks, both PASS (t50/40: 6 cm ball, lift 8 cm, carry 0.23 m, 420 ticks; t49/40: 7 cm fruit, lift 17 cm, carry 0.26 m, 459 ticks). The same closure held a lying 3.6 cm rod through its lift (t34/40); it was lost in a later free, turning carry (N2). A closure made of separate dwells never came to a lift (N4). **STATUS: supported**

## Proposed (worked once)

### P1. Colour-aimed shallow press on a flush switch
- WHEN: a small painted or flush control with no depth relief must be pressed; a pixel read by eye can lie beside it.
- DO: in a zero-tick step mask its colour in the head picture, take the centroid pixel, `point_at` it and check that its height is the object's top; `solve_ik` above it with `hand='point'`; ONE press of 3 mm at 0.03 m/s that stays 0.3 s; look; never press again once the effect shows (it may be a toggle).
```python
rgb = view.cameras['head'].rgb; R = rgb[:, :, 0].astype(float); G = rgb[:, :, 1].astype(float); B = rgb[:, :, 2].astype(float)
vs, us = ((R > 120) & (G < 0.62 * R) & (B < 0.62 * R)).nonzero()        # a red patch; keep only pixels inside the object's box
p = point_at(view, float(us.mean()), float(vs.mean()))
r = guarded_press(view, Pose(p.point, palm_down_pose(p.point).quaternion_wxyz, 'world'), max_travel_m=0.003, speed_m_s=0.03, stay_s=0.3)
```
- CHECK: `picture_brightness_change` clearly positive, or region means of the rgb array before and after (the guard never stops and the index force reads 0 on a light thing); from a clean view the thing was not shoved.
- EVIDENCE: worked 1 of 1 (t41/40, PASS: the first 3 mm press, 465 ticks for the whole task). Aimed by eye 6 mm below the top, presses of 3 and 8 mm did nothing (t41/0); a shallow press on the body of a lying torch did not switch it (t35/0). **STATUS: proposed**

## Contradicted: what did not work, and what to do instead

### N1. Bare finger where a tool lies beside the work
- WHEN: the instruction names a tool action (strike, tap, unscrew, scrape, crush) or a tool lies next to the part, and the part is held by something (a screw in its thread, a pressed-on lid).
- DO instead: in the first zero-tick steps ask a grasp for the tool's own mask part (S3) and `solve_ik` its working pose with every candidate's hand orientation; count the tool route's ticks before anything else. At most ONE probe: `guarded_press(view, target, max_travel_m=0.003, retract=False)` (palm height against the plan and the arm's joint lag show whether the part yields). After one contact without effect change the skill, never the depth, speed or sense.
- CHECK: the part's pose from depth before and after (plane fit, axis of a slot): equal pixel for pixel means nothing moved.
- EVIDENCE: worked 0 of 4 on 3 tasks (t07/40: four fingertip programs on a screw at 2 to 14 N, 2864 ticks; t03/40: presses to 60 N, taps at 0.28 m/s and a sideways pull, 1301 ticks; t40/40, t40/41: a tool tipped flat and pushed with the fingertips did the visible job, status FAIL twice). 8 repeats of a contact that had changed nothing changed nothing (t07/40: 3; t03/40: 4; t41/0: 1). The tool route has not passed either: no grasp held a hammer (N9; its pick clip with a 1.9 cm flange raise lost it as the lift ended in t02/40 and t01/40, the other offered clip is untried), a held rod was lost after its first press (P5). **STATUS: contradicted**
### N2. Quarter turn of a loaded hand, then carry
- WHEN: the held thing sits wrongly for the place and a turn of the hand by 60 degrees or more before or during the carry looks like the fix; also `strike`, which turns the hand until the tool's long axis points along the blow.
- DO instead: choose the grasp turn for the place before grasping (S2); carry in the grasp's own orientation; a needed turn stays under 40 degrees as one level orbit (S13), else set down and grasp again; a pinch of your own is carried by linear segments at 0.12 m/s without turning.
- CHECK: fingertip forces after the turn; contact that moved from the fingertips to the inner finger links: do not carry.
- EVIDENCE: worked 0 of 3 on 3 tasks (t27/40: `wrist_reorient` by 90 degrees kept the thing, the `transport` after it lost it within 0.5 s; t34/40: a free carry with a 75 degree turn at 0.20 m/s lost a pinch off the table; t02/40: a 25 degree roll in a fast descent threw the tool out). `transport` keeps the hand's orientation only at its end (28 degrees of pitch on the way, t27/40). **STATUS: contradicted**
### N3. Fist push or slide to nudge a small block
- WHEN: a free block of about hand size landed short of its place or stands cornered, and one `push` or `slide` looks cheaper than a grasp.
