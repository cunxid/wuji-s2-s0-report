What held up

  • The real-time physics fix — the co-sim had been advancing 8ms of physics per 20ms control tick (0.4× time) since its creation. Fixing it invalidated nearly every tuned behavior; strike/press/marker/handoff were requalified with measured root causes (in-hand grip creep has a ~5s deadline; the press target orientation was IK-infeasible; handoffs need a tablecloth retreat because open fingers form a shelf).
  • Perception-only operation — SAM 2.1 segmentation (SAM 3 is license-gated; disclosed) + mesh-ICP 6-DoF pose + rendered depth height maps; the robot self-filters via its own kinematic mask; the collision-aware carry planner now consumes the perceived depth scan by default (A/B bit-identical on clear scenes: place 1.2mm both ways).
  • Original-code scoring — HandsOn's success predicates ported with per-constant file:line citations (t24 ball-to-bowl passes all seven official terms at 15.96mm); DexJoCo scored by running their unmodified env classes under their pinned dependencies (numpy 1.26.4 / mujoco 3.4.0) via zero-substep replay of our measured traces — their hammer_nail code returns succeed: true (nail 0.051m ≥ 0.04) on our episode.
  • The probed envelope — hammerS tracks 0.375–0.5 m/s translations in every direction, 75–112° arcs, 120° yaw; box60 the same minus +y / diagonal / yaw (grip-geometry walls); the measured carry-speed floor (88 ticks) sits below the wrappers' shipped 150–220, so the envelope certifies existing speeds rather than clamping them.
  • IK that doesn't fly — a 944-log diagnosis found three divergence modes (inner-workspace retreats, near-column branch thrash, out-of-reach replan targets); the gated recovery is bit-identical when healthy and cuts the worst oscillations from 1005mm to recovering under 17mm; retreat endpoints and replan targets are clamped to the workspace shell.

What broke, and what it taught

  • The capability atlas poisoned its own system — stale slow-motion cells seeded dials (SPIN_DEG −90, SQUEEZE 2.5) that overrode requalified configs and zeroed a working strike. Memory without a validity domain is a liability: atlas seeding is disabled until every cell carries the real-time tag, and builder configs now take precedence.
  • Self-judged success inflates — the first full evaluation scored 16/36 by letting S2's perception judgment claim PASS ("something moved"); re-scored under official-else-grader it was 4/36. The page now shows S2's judgment as chips only.
  • Thin-vessel retention is a genuine S0 gap — cyl30x80 slides through the fingertip pinch ~1.3mm/tick whenever the wrist translates (retention ceiling 1.4–2s); fourteen mitigations measured, none sufficient. Sphere and thin-cylinder capture classes are untrackable across the whole probe grid. This is the clearest training-data target for S0.
  • Official bimanual terms don't forgive — official t08 requires the wood block lifted 30mm by the second hand; our left-arm lift machine broke at real-time and is still flagged. The depth term passes (16mm ≥ 6); the row stays FAIL until the lift requalifies.
  • Seven shipped arenas contain no task objects (deformables and two composites — assets are runtime-injected by HandsOn's IsaacLab stack, which also means HandsOn's production physics is PhysX: engine parity from MuJoCo is impossible, so "original" means their merged scene MJCFs, stepping contract, budgets, predicates, and camera spec — all adopted).
