Findings

  • Articulated-object verbs dominate the gap: open + close + turn-knob + screw account for ~71 step-demands. The registry is strong on free objects, blind on kinematic trees — yet the press verb's hinge grader means the measurement half is already built.
  • The easy tier is large: close-articulated, pull, push, stack, reorient, wipe, squeeze (~63 step-demands) are pure reference-rewriting with mostly existing dials/clips.
  • Zero demand for toss/shake/stir across all 229 tasks — deprioritized despite being easy.
  • Meta-World is the harshest benchmark for this registry (push/pull/articulated-heavy, ~30% covered); BEHAVIOR-100 is friendlier — long-horizon but mostly place chains gated on open-articulated.
