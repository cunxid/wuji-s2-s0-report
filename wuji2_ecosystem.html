Every wuji2-compatible benchmark we could find

Headline correction to our own scope: HandsOn is wuji2-native for 50 of its 67 registered tasks (their loader source_path_for_hand('wuji2'); several tasks share one arena, so the 37 shipped wuji2 arena files in our verified gallery underpin 50 tasks). And official wuji2 trajectory data exists: Cyan666/HandsOn-Datasets carries a newton_wuji2 subset (10k raw trajectories; 200 raw files + examples verified on HF).

Tier 2 — pluggable benchmark + the official wuji2 model makes a port straightforward

The vendor ships wuji-description (MIT): Wuji Hand 2.1/2.2, URDF + MJCF + USD — our rokae_wuji2 includes are provably vendored from it (HandsOn's own vendor_wuji2_model.py). Viable targets: DexVerse (IsaacLab, BSD-3; wuji gen-1 community assets exist), ManiSkill (custom-robot tutorial), DexJoCo (the easiest — HandsOn's wuji2 arenas ARE DexJoCo's arenas with the robot swapped; DexJoCo itself ships Panda+Allegro only, which is why it was removed from our wuji2 deliverables).

Tier 3 — nothing found

MuJoCo Menagerie (no wuji model), MuJoCo Playground, Bi-DexHands, RoboHive, RoboCasa, DexMimicGen, GenDexHand: no wuji/wuji2 presence by any repo-level signal.
